Privacy

This site belongs to Buy One Media, Dayton, Ohio, publisher of THE ONLY CONCLUSION from Brain Focus. It asks for very little, and this page says what happens to it.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form: your email address, and on the Case Zero pages the name you want on your letter and, if you give it, your line number. We don't use advertising trackers. Our web host keeps ordinary server logs for security.

What we do with it

  • We send the letter or answer you asked for.
  • We write when the next case opens. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and one click takes you off.
  • We never sell or share your address.

Who handles it

Our mailing list is kept by an email service that stores addresses on our behalf. The free master sheets are plain downloads and ask for nothing.

Taking your name off

Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or reply to one and ask us to delete your details. We will.
